Покупки

Набор монет и «Комплект игрока» покупаются через Google Play. Оплату принимает и обрабатывает Google: мы не получаем и не видим номер карты, имя плательщика и другие платёжные данные. Перенос прогресса и резервная копия

Файл. Кнопки «Сохранить в файл» и «Загрузить из файла» в настройках игры создают и читают файл с вашим прогрессом — только когда вы сами их нажали. Куда отправить этот файл (в облако, в мессенджер, на другой телефон), вы решаете сами через системное меню; приложение никуда его не отправляет.

Резервная копия Android. Если на телефоне включена резервная копия Google, система может копировать в ваш аккаунт Google базу с прогрессом и настройки игры и вернуть их при установке на новый телефон. Это делает Android по правилам Google, а не мы; отключить это можно в настройках телефона: Настройки → Google → Резервное копирование.

Кнопка «Поделиться»

Она открывает системное меню с коротким текстом и ссылкой на игру. Приложение само ничего не отправляет: получателя выбираете вы.

Произношение слов

В разделе «Мой словарь» слово можно послушать. Произносит его не наше приложение, а синтезатор речи, встроенный в устройство, — слово передаётся ему. На Android это обычно «Речевые сервисы Google»: если нужный голос не загружен на устройство, сервис может обработать текст на своих серверах. Мы этот текст не получаем и не храним.

Сеть и разрешения

Игра работает без интернета. Сеть нужна только для рекламы, покупок и, если голос не загружен, для синтезатора речи. Приложение не запрашивает доступ к контактам, камере, микрофону, геолокации, фотографиям и файлам вашего устройства.

In short

The game needs no account or sign-up, has no server of its own, and we neither collect nor see your personal data. Your progress is stored on your device. The third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases) and your device's text-to-speech engine (word pronunciation).

What is stored on your device

  • completed levels, stars and your best time without hints;
  • coins, found words, trophies, purchased upgrades and looks;
  • daily rewards, quests and the day streak;
  • the name and avatar you chose yourself;
  • the game language and your settings.

This data lives in the device's own storage. We do not receive or see it: the game has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. The name you enter stays on your device and is sent nowhere.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of the screen while you play a level — it appears on its own;
  • a rewarded video — only when you tap the button yourself to get hints or coins.

There are no full-screen ads between levels at all. Any purchase in the app (a coin set or the Player pack) removes the banner.

To serve an ad, Google may receive:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • device details: model, operating system version, language;
  • the IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad events: impression, view, click.

Consent. In countries where GDPR applies (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland) a Google consent form is shown on first launch. Personalised ads are switched on there only if you allow them, and no ads are requested before you answer. You can change your choice at any time: game Settings → "Ad settings" (the item appears where the form applies). In other countries the ads may be personalised; you can opt out in Android settings (see below).

How Google collects and uses this data is described here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Coin sets and the Player pack are bought through Google Play. Google takes and processes the payment: we do not receive or see your card number, the payer's name or other payment details. To restore what you bought (for example after a reinstall), the app asks Google Play which purchases your account has; the purchase confirmation is used only on the device, and nothing is stored on any server of ours.

Moving your progress and backups

File. The "Save to a file" and "Load from a file" buttons in the game's settings create and read a file with your progress — only when you tap them yourself. Where the file goes (cloud storage, a messenger, another phone) is your choice through the system menu; the app sends it nowhere.

Android backup. If Google backup is on for your phone, the system may copy the game's progress database and settings to your Google account and restore them when you set up a new phone. This is done by Android under Google's rules, not by us; you can turn it off in phone settings: Settings → Google → Backup.

The "Share" button

It opens the system share menu with a short text and a link to the game. The app itself sends nothing: you choose the recipient.

Word pronunciation

In "My dictionary" you can listen to a word. The speech is produced not by our app but by the text-to-speech engine built into your device, and the word is passed to that engine. On Android this is usually Google Speech Services, which may process the text on its own servers when the voice is not downloaded to the device. We neither receive nor store that text.

Network and permissions

The game works offline. The network is needed only for ads, purchases and, when a voice is not downloaded, for the speech engine. The app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on the device until you tap "Clear results" in settings or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google account (if backup is on) can be deleted in Google's backup settings. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own rules.

Your choices

  • Reset or fully switch off the advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some phones Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Change your ad consent: game Settings → "Ad settings".
  • Erase your progress: "Clear results" in the game's settings.
  • Questions about your data — write to the address below. We hold no personal data of yours, so requests about advertising data must go to Google.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Iklan

Aplikasi menampilkan iklan melalui Google AdMob:

  • spanduk di bagian bawah layar saat bermain level — muncul dengan sendirinya;
  • video berhadiah — hanya saat Anda sendiri menekan tombol untuk mendapatkan petunjuk atau koin.

Tidak ada iklan layar penuh di antara level. Setiap pembelian di aplikasi (paket koin atau «Paket pemain») menghilangkan spanduk.

Untuk menayangkan iklan, Google dapat menerima:

  • ID iklan perangkat (Advertising ID);
  • informasi perangkat: model, versi sistem operasi, bahasa;
  • alamat IP dan perkiraan lokasi yang diturunkan darinya;
  • peristiwa iklan: tayangan, tontonan, klik.

Persetujuan. Di negara yang menerapkan GDPR (Wilayah Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, Swiss), formulir persetujuan Google ditampilkan saat pertama kali dibuka. Di sana iklan yang dipersonalisasi baru aktif jika Anda mengizinkan, dan tidak ada iklan yang diminta sebelum Anda menjawab. Anda dapat mengubah pilihan kapan saja: Setelan permainan → «Setelan iklan» (opsi ini muncul di tempat formulir berlaku). Di negara lain iklan dapat dipersonalisasi; Anda dapat menonaktifkannya di setelan Android (lihat di bawah).

Berkas. Tombol «Simpan ke berkas» dan «Muat dari berkas» di setelan permainan membuat dan membaca berkas berisi kemajuan Anda — hanya saat Anda sendiri menekannya. Ke mana berkas itu dikirim (penyimpanan awan, aplikasi pesan, ponsel lain) Anda tentukan sendiri lewat menu sistem; aplikasi tidak mengirimnya ke mana pun.

Pencadangan Android. Jika pencadangan Google aktif di ponsel Anda, sistem dapat menyalin basis data kemajuan dan setelan permainan ke akun Google Anda dan memulihkannya di ponsel baru. Ini dilakukan Android menurut aturan Google, bukan kami; Anda dapat menonaktifkannya di setelan ponsel: Setelan → Google → Pencadangan.

Tombol ini membuka menu berbagi sistem dengan teks singkat dan tautan ke permainan. Aplikasi sendiri tidak mengirim apa pun: penerimanya Anda yang memilih.

Masa penyimpanan dan penghapusan

Data permainan tetap di perangkat sampai Anda menekan «Hapus hasil» di setelan atau mencopot aplikasi. Salinan di akun Google Anda (jika pencadangan aktif) dapat dihapus di setelan pencadangan Google. Data iklan disimpan Google menurut aturannya sendiri.

Yang dapat Anda lakukan

  • Mengatur ulang atau menonaktifkan sepenuhnya ID iklan: Setelan Android → Privasi → Iklan (di sebagian ponsel Setelan → Google → Iklan).
  • Mengubah persetujuan iklan: Setelan permainan → «Setelan iklan».
  • Menghapus kemajuan: «Hapus hasil» di setelan permainan.
  • Pertanyaan tentang data Anda — tulis ke alamat di bawah. Kami tidak menyimpan data pribadi Anda, sehingga permintaan tentang data iklan harus ditujukan kepada Google.
